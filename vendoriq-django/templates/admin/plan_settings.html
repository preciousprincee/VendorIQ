Read-only - this mirrors apps/billing/plans.py, the single source of truth the app itself reads for pricing, limits, and feature access. Plan limits and features are code-driven (nothing here is stored in the database for those), so change those in that file, then redeploy. Price and free trial length are the two exceptions - both are live, database-backed settings you can change below with no redeploy, and both take effect immediately, including for accounts already paying or mid-trial.

Pro plan price

Current price

₦{{ pro_price|floatformat:0 }}/mo

This is a live setting, not a code/environment value - change it from Billing › Pro plan price and it takes effect immediately for every new upgrade (Subscription page, upgrade modal, this page all show the new price right away). It does not retroactively change what someone already paying is charged - each subscriber locks in the price shown at the moment they upgrade (see Subscription.price_ngn in apps/billing/models.py), so MRR on the Revenue page reflects what people actually agreed to pay, not subscriber count × today's price.
