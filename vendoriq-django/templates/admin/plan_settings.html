Read-only - this mirrors apps/billing/plans.py, the single source of truth the app itself reads for pricing, limits, and feature access. Plan limits and features are code-driven (nothing here is stored in the database for those), so change those in that file, then redeploy. Price and free trial length are the two exceptions - both are live, database-backed settings you can change below with no redeploy, and both take effect immediately, including for accounts already paying or mid-trial.

These are live, database-backed settings, not code/environment values - change any of them from Billing › Plan prices by opening the cycle you want to change and editing its price directly (or use "Add plan price" to record a dated new version instead, if you'd rather keep an explicit change log). Either way it takes effect immediately for every new purchase (pricing page, checkout, this page all show the new price right away). It does not retroactively change what someone already paying is charged - each subscriber locks in the price at the moment they purchase/renew (see Subscription.price_ngn and SubscriptionPurchase in apps/billing/models.py), so MRR on the Revenue page reflects what people actually agreed to pay, not subscriber count × today's price. Every historical purchase remains untouched regardless of later price changes - see Subscription purchases.
